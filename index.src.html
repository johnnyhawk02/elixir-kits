saved kits

    Export saves every kit here as elixir-kits.json. On Elixir Mobile, open PRST and press IMPORT to add them as presets (sound only: the phone keeps its own pattern). A .snd file is one kit in the LXR-02's own format.

    keys: space play/stop · k new kit · p new pattern · u undo · s save · 1-6 play a drum · ← → preset · r random preset. drag across the grid to paint steps.
